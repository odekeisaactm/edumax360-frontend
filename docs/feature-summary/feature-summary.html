Teaching & Assessment

The core of the platform: how lessons are prepared, exams are set and delivered, and scores are produced.

01

Assessment & Computer-Based Testing

Flagship

A complete exam workflow — from building question banks to marking and publishing — with strong controls against malpractice.

  • Rich question banks with equations, symbols, graphs and diagrams; bulk upload from Excel.
  • AI question generation from a subject and topic, reviewed by the teacher before use.
  • Exam scheduling by class, arm and subject, with exam halls, timing windows and question requirements.
  • Secure entry with student exam PINs (printable) and approved exam devices.
  • Dedicated exam apps for Windows and Android that lock the device to the exam and detect app switching, split-screen, overlays and screen capture.
  • AI webcam proctoring: detects no face, multiple faces and a different person from the student's photo; periodic snapshots for review.
  • Live invigilation dashboard: see who is online, send warnings, grant extra time, or end a student's exam.
  • Never lose an answer: answers are kept on the device and auto-saved; a student can resume after a power cut or on another computer.
  • Students answer theory with maths and graphs, not just plain text.
  • Instant objective marking and AI marking of theory, with teacher review for low-confidence answers.
  • Scanned paper exams: upload answer sheets for marking alongside CBT.
  • One-click transfer of exam scores into the results module.
In practice: a student's power goes off mid-exam. They log in again and continue exactly where they stopped — same questions, same answers, the timer still fair.
02

Learning Resources

AI-assisted

Helps teachers plan and prepare quality lessons faster, while giving school leaders oversight of what is taught.

  • Schemes of work planned week by week, including non-teaching weeks; import from an existing document.
  • AI lesson note writing from a topic and learning objectives.
  • AI rewrite of a whole note or a selected paragraph, following the teacher's instruction.
  • AI vetting checks a note's quality before it goes for approval, with feedback for the teacher.
  • Approval workflow for notes and schemes, with optional auto-approval.
  • AI summaries and text-to-speech audio of notes; PDF download; control over what students can access.

Results & Money

Accurate report cards and complete visibility of every naira the school bills, receives and spends.

03

Results & Report Cards

  • Fully configurable score fields, grading scales, behaviour ratings and comment templates.
  • Score entry by subject teachers, with tracking of who has and hasn't uploaded.
  • Automatic computation of totals, grades, positions and statistics.
  • Teacher and principal comments, individually or in bulk from templates.
  • Controlled publishing to parents, and printable report cards.
  • Broadsheets, cumulative results, prize lists, analytics and a full results archive.
04

Fees & Billing

  • Flexible fee structures by class, group and term; bulk invoice generation.
  • Family invoices so parents pay for all their children in one place.
  • Discounts and waivers, including class-based discount tiers.
  • Online payment through integrated payment gateways, with automatic receipts.
  • Debtor tracking and payment reminders.
  • Safe corrections to issued invoices, with a full history.
05

Finance & Wallets

  • Income and expense recording by category, with bank accounts and deposits.
  • Digital wallets for students, parents and staff, with transfers and full ledgers.
  • Supplier payments and advance payments.
  • Financial dashboard and reports.
In practice: a parent funds a wallet once and uses it for fees and school-shop purchases, and the bursar sees every transaction in one ledger.

Attendance & Gate
